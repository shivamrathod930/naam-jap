Privacy

Your practice stays yours.

Naam Jap is designed as an offline-first app. It does not require an account or a server to count your daily naam jap.

  • No account or login is required.
  • No advertising profile is created.
  • The app is intended to keep practice data on the device.
  • Internet access is not required for the core counting experience.
