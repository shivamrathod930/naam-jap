Naam Jap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Naam Jap is a simple, offline-first mantra counting application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app is designed to collect, use, and store.

1. Information We Collect

Naam Jap does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. App Usage Data

The core purpose of the app is to let you select a mantra, set a daily target, and count repetitions. These settings and counts are intended to remain on your device and are not sent to a remote server for the core app experience.

3. Internet and Third Parties

The core counting functionality does not require an internet connection. The app is not designed to sell or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Security

No online account or cloud database is required for the core experience. However, no software can guarantee absolute security, so you should keep your device protected with its normal security features.

5. Children's Privacy

Naam Jap does not knowingly request personal information from children. Because the app is designed to operate without an account, the core experience does not require a child to submit personal information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the official app listing or website.

8. Important Note

This policy describes the intended privacy behavior of the current offline-first version of Naam Jap. If future versions add analytics, advertising, cloud sync, login, notifications through external services, or other third-party SDKs, this policy should be updated before those features are released.
